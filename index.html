About Helping Handz

Helping Handz is a charity organization in North Dakota dedicated to collecting winter coats for children. Our mission is to help students stay warm, comfortable, and ready for school during the cold winter season.

Who We Help

We support children from Kindergarten to Grade 12.

🧒

Young Children

Providing warm coats for children in elementary school.

🎒

Students

Helping students stay comfortable while going to school.

Our Community
